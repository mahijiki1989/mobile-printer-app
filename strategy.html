🧠 Strategy Logic — Hindi Mein Samjho

📈 Higher Timeframe = Bias Decide Karta Hai

30 min chart par RSI dekho. Agar RSI < 60 hai matlab market abhi strong nahi hai — yahan se hum sell side ya weakness dhoondhenge.

🔑 Yaad Rakho:

  • 30m RSI < 60 = Trend bearish/sideways → PUT side play
  • 30m RSI > 60 = Trend bullish → CALL side dekho (mirror logic)
  • Higher TF = boss, lower TF = trigger only

⚡ Lower Timeframe = Entry Trigger

10 min chart par wait karo. RSI ne 60 ko break kiya upar (false breakout), fir wapas 60 ke andar aaya, aur candle close hua — yahi entry trigger hai.

🔑 Trigger Recipe:

  • Step 1: 10m RSI 60 ke upar gaya (poke)
  • Step 2: Wapas 60 ke neeche aaya
  • Step 3: Candle 60 ke neeche close hua
  • Step 4: Us candle ke close pe SHORT entry

🪜 Step by Step — Trade Kaise Lena Hai

1

🔍 30-Min Chart Open Karo

Sabse pehle Nifty ya Bank Nifty ka 30 minute timeframe kholo. RSI(14) indicator lagao. Dekho RSI value kya hai.

⚠️ Agar 30m RSI 60 ke neeche hai → Aage badho. Agar upar hai → Yeh setup nahi banta, wait karo.
2

⚡ 10-Min Chart Pe Switch Karo

Ab same chart ka 10 minute timeframe kholo. Yahan bhi RSI(14) lagao. Dhyan se dekho ki RSI 60 ke saath kaisa behave kar raha hai.

👀 Wait karo jab tak RSI 60 ko touch/break kare
3

🎯 Entry Confirmation

RSI 60 ke upar gaya ek-do candle ke liye, fir wapas 60 ke neeche aaya, aur us candle ka close 60 ke neeche hua. YEH HI ENTRY HAI!

✅ Entry: Us 10min candle ke close pe SHORT (PUT buy karo / Future sell)
4

🛡️ Stop Loss Lagao

SL = Recent Swing High (10 min chart pe last bana hua high). Ye fixed SL hai jo trade open hote hi lagana hai. Mental SL mat rakhna, broker me actual order daalna.

📍 SL = Swing High + 2-3 points buffer (false hit avoid karne ke liye)
5

🏆 Target Set Karo (1:2 ya 1:3)

Risk count karo: SL Distance = Entry – Swing High (absolute value). Ab target = Entry – (2 × SL distance) ya (3 × SL distance).

💡 Tip: 50% qty 1:2 pe book, baki 50% trailing SL ke saath 1:3 tak chalao

⚖️ Karna Hai vs Nahi Karna

✅ Yeh Zaroor Karo

  • Pehle 30m bias check karo, fir hi 10m dekho
  • Candle close ka wait karo (mid-candle entry NHI)
  • SL hamesha actual broker me lagao
  • 1 day me max 2-3 trades — quality > quantity
  • Trade journal maintain karo har trade ka
  • Risk per trade = capital ka 1-2% only
  • 3:15 PM tak square off mandatory
  • News events (RBI, Fed, Budget) ke time avoid

❌ Yeh Bilkul Mat Karo

  • Mid-candle entry mat lo — patience rakho
  • SL ko hatake "hope" pe trade mat chalao
  • Position double mat karo loss me (averaging)
  • Ek hi din 5+ trades mat lo (overtrading)
  • 30m bias against ja raha ho to mat lo
  • Sirf RSI dekh ke trade mat lo, price action bhi
  • Overnight position mat hold karo
  • Loss recovery ke chakkar me revenge trading mat
